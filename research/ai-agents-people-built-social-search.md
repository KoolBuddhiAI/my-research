## Observations
- The most common real-world agent types: **sales outreach and lead response**,
  **marketing and social replies**, **customer churn and support**, and **agencies run by agents**.
- Common tools: **n8n**, **Make.com**, **Claude**, and open-source multi-agent frameworks.
- Threads posts gave the most concrete detail on how agents were built. X gave the most
  revenue stories. Instagram is weak through search engines, and the official API or the app works better there.
